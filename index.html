And somehow in your own beautiful way you became that person to me❤️...I want you to know that beyond all the sweet words, the jokes, the teasing, the little moments and the affection I genuinely want to see you win in life...I want to see you grow into a woman who knows her worth trusts her abilities, protects her peace and never settles for a life that makes her forget how beautiful it is to be alive...I want to see you achieve the things you once thought were impossible. I want to celebrate your good days with you remind you of your strength on your difficult days and encourage you whenever the road ahead feels too long...I want you to feel safe enough to be yourself around me without having to pretend to be strong every second or hide the parts of you that are still healing...You deserve to be loved in a way that makes room for your humanity,,your imperfections, your fears, your ambitions, your laughter and all the little things that make you uniquely YOU....My kimooda never feel like you have to become somebody else to deserve good things. You do not have to be perfect to be appreciated. You do not have to be happy all the time to be loved. You do not have to earn your right to rest by exhausting yourself first. And you certainly do not have to diminish your light to make other people comfortable. There is room in this world for your dreams, your voice, your personality, your kindness, your softness, your strength, and everything else that makes you who you are. Please keep being that girl who laughs, who dreams, who learns, who hopes, and who keeps discovering the world. Protect the beautiful heart you carry, but do not let the world convince you that having a beautiful heart is a weakness❤️🌷..As you turn nineteen, I pray that you experience a love that brings you peace rather than confusion, friendships that feel genuine rather than forced, opportunities that help you grow rather than shrink, and moments that remind you that life is still worth looking forward to. Do not be afraid of dreaming bigger. Do not be afraid to walk away from what no longer serves you or to begin again when life calls you to. You are allowed to outgrow old versions of yourself. You are allowed to choose peace. You are allowed to become someone your younger self would look at with pride.
